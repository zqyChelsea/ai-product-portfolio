# Route planning

Treat must-sees, dates, departure airport, transport and daily pace as hard constraints. Cluster stops by district before ordering; compare verified transit legs when available. Reserve arrival and departure buffers, and do not schedule a full-day theme park with several other major attractions. A nearby city is optional and should consume at most one day for this five-day case. If a transit leg is not verified, show a route hypothesis with no fabricated duration/fare. Expose trade-offs: why a stop was moved, which selection could not be placed, and whether the trip exceeds the traveler's budget. Keep the agent's prose advisory; deterministic validation owns factual fields.
